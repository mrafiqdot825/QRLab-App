## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

**QRLab** is built with privacy-by-design. We believe your data belongs to you. **All QR code generation, customization, payload rendering, and code scanning operate 100% locally and privately on your physical device.**

We do not operate remote databases, servers, or cloud repositories that collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **None.** We do not ask for, collect, process, or sell any personal identifying information (PII) such as your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or location.

### B. Generated Data & Payloads
- Any data you type into the generator (such as URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, vCard contacts, email addresses, or plain text notes) is processed **in-memory and stored exclusively on your device's local sandboxed storage**.
- Your data is never transmitted across the internet to any external server.

### C. Camera & Scanning
- If you use the QR scanner feature, the app requires access to your device camera solely to read optical barcodes in real time.
- The camera stream is analyzed on-device; video feeds and captured images are never uploaded, recorded, or shared.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

QRLab requests only the minimal set of hardware permissions required for its features:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `android.permission.CAMERA` | Used solely for optical real-time scanning of QR codes through the viewfinder. |
| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides subtle haptic feedback during UI interactions and successful scans. |
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Used standardly for network capabilities (such as opening links that you tap on) and Expo runtime initialization. |

> **Note:** QRLab does **not** access your contacts, microphone, location, photo gallery, or SMS.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

- **No Behavioral Trackers:** We do not integrate third-party behavioral analytics (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or Facebook SDK).
- **No Advertising:** QRLab does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not track your advertising identifier (AAID / IDFA).

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all app data and history are stored locally on your device:
- You have complete control over your data.
- Clearing the app cache/data in Android Settings or uninstalling QRLab permanently removes all locally stored data immediately.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

QRLab does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages and complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
